About the Conference

This international conference brings together experts from various fields to discuss intellectual property rights, climate action, and sustainable development.

Friday, 11 July 2026

  • Arabic

Conference Program

Day 1 (July 10)
Day 2 (July 11)

Day 1 – Thursday, 10 July 2025

Theme: Intellectual Property Flexibilities & Climate Imperatives

15:00 – 15:30
Opening Session

Welcome from Organizing Institutions
Introduction to the Conference Theme

15:30 – 16:30
Keynote Addresses

"The Doha Declaration and Climate Justice: Lessons for Sustainability"
"From TRIPS to Technology Sharing: Intellectual Property and the Green Transition"

16:30 – 17:30
Plenary Dialogue

Panel 1: Navigating IPR Flexibilities for Climate-Resilient Technologies
Contributions from legal experts, environmental engineers, and IT scientists.

17:30 – 18:00
Networking Break (Hybrid)
18:00 – 19:30
Thematic Parallel Sessions

Track A (Biologists & Biodiversity Specialists): Climate Change, Biodiversity, and IPR Challenges in the Mediterranean

Track B (Chemists & Physicists): Green Chemistry and Energy Transitions: Patents and Knowledge Commons

Track C (Mathematicians & IT Scientists): Modeling Climate Futures & Data-Sharing Rights

19:30 – 20:00
Day 1 Wrap-Up & Synthesis

Day 2 – Friday, 11 July 2025

Theme: Regional Cooperation, Science, and Technology Transfer

09:00 – 09:30
Morning Keynote

"Climate Action through Scientific Collaboration: Iberia & North Africa"

09:30 – 11:00
Plenary Panel

Panel 2: Bridging Science and Policy – From Laboratories to Climate Agreements
Interdisciplinary interventions (biologists, physicists, chemists, environmental engineers).

11:00 – 11:30
Coffee Break
11:30 – 13:00
Thematic Parallel Sessions

Track A (Legal & Policy Focus): IPR Flexibilities in Renewable Energy & Water Technologies

Track B (Scientific Focus): Frontiers of Carbon Capture, Storage, and Clean Tech

Track C (Regional Cooperation): Mediterranean Innovation Networks: Spain, Portugal & North Africa

13:00 – 14:30
Lunch Break
14:30 – 16:00
Special Roundtable

Young Researchers Forum – PhD Students from Euro-Mediterranean Universities
(Interactive session for emerging scholars to present posters and lightning talks).

16:00 – 16:30
Break
16:30 – 17:30
High-Level Closing Panel

“Towards a Sustainable Future: Integrating IPR Flexibilities, Scientific Innovation, and Regional Cooperation”

17:30 – 18:00
Closing Remarks & Adoption of the "Barcelona-Madrid Declaration on IPRs and Climate Action"
